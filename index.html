How a project works

Start with one useful piece.

A stadium does not need to digitise everything at once. A focused pilot can prove the workflow before scaling across the venue.

01 / DISCOVER

Define the use case

Agree on the audience, goals and available source material.

02 / STRUCTURE

Prepare the data

Organise venue plans, seating references and section information.

03 / BUILD

Create the experience

Design the interactive map and visual layers around the venue.

04 / VERIFY

Review with the venue

Validate the information before publishing or operational use.

About StadiaFrame

"Make the entire venue understandable — from the full seating bowl to a single seat."

Built around clarity.

StadiaFrame is a South African venture focused on interactive venue mapping, digital stadium visualisation and structured seating information.

The aim is simple: take the complexity of a stadium and present it through a clear digital interface that can support stadium teams, event planners and visitors.
